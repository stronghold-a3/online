Stronghold A3 Resilience Model
C + A − S − E

Coping Capacity + Adaptability − Sensitivity − Exposure

C

Coping Capacity

Prepare, respond, absorb disruption, and maintain essential functions.

A

Adaptability

Learn, adjust, improve controls, and adapt to changing risks.

S

Sensitivity

Identify conditions that can increase the impact of disruption.

E

Exposure

Identify hazards, threats, dependencies, and conditions that increase risk.
