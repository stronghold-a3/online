Safe Schools Act Alignment

A school safety lens built around readiness and resilience

The assessment is structured around safe-school priorities including preparedness, emergency response, physical safety, safeguarding, digital risks, continuity, and institutional improvement. It is an assessment aid, not a government certification or legal determination of compliance.

Stronghold A3 Mission

Translate risk information into stronger institutional resilience, preparedness, protection, and response capability.

Education Focus

Considers students, staff, facilities, digital systems, critical functions, families, and community response.
