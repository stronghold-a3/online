02 · Student & behavioral safety

Are people and behavior risks actively managed?

03 · Digital safety

Can the school protect systems, data, and people online?

04 · Emergency readiness

Can the school respond quickly and recover well?

Assess the system as it exists today: alerts, roles, drills, routes, communications, and recovery.
