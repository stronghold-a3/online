Four domains, one equation

Two domains measure what your school can do. Two measure what your school is carrying. The gap between them is resilience.

C

Coping Capacity

What the school can do on the day: guards on post, plans that exist, comms that reach people, functions that survive.

Higher is better · adds to resilience

A

Adaptability

Whether lessons stick: reviews, corrective actions with owners, updated post orders, tested controls.

Higher is better · adds to resilience

S

Sensitivity

Conditions that magnify harm: single points of failure, learners needing support, records in one place, no escalation path.

Higher is worse · subtracts from resilience

E

Exposure

What the campus is up against: open perimeters, unscreened entry, flood and storm geography, cyber and cash risk.

Higher is worse · subtracts from resilience

C and A are answered on a capability scale, where 3 means documented, practised and tested. S and E are answered on a pressure scale, where 3 means the condition is present and unmanaged. That is why answering honestly about a weakness lowers the index rather than raising it.
